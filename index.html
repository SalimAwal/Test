About the day

Code & Tea started as six students sharing one laptop charger in a library. It is now a day of short talks and one hands-on workshop, run by students for students, with tea every ninety minutes.

Every talk is aimed at someone who has built their first page and wants to build a better second one. No talk assumes a framework.

What you get

  • Four twenty-minute talks from working developers
  • A ninety-minute workshop: deploy a site before lunch
  • Lunch, and more tea than is strictly sensible
  • A certificate of attendance
